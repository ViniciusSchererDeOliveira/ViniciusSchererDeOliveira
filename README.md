### [Asterism Polyphony](https://github.com/ViniciusSchererDeOliveira/asterism-polyphony) — [`v1.0.1 · Days`](https://github.com/ViniciusSchererDeOliveira/asterism-polyphony/releases/tag/v1.0.1)

**A deterministic all-fourths guitar voicing engine and visual harmonic atlas.**

Polyphony began as a tool to make learning guitar easier. All-fourths tuning turns the fretboard into a regular lattice, while chess-piece routes make harmonic movements visual and repeatable. The engine searches for playable voicings under explicit musical and physical constraints, ranks them transparently, and shows why one result may fit better than another.

Built as a TypeScript workspace with a dependency-free computational core, a React interface, browser-worker integration, reproducible outputs, and automated core, integration, SSR, and browser tests.

## How I build

- **Local-first when practical.** Core workflows should remain inspectable, portable, and useful without depending on a hosted black box.
- **Deterministic at the core.** Models may interpret and advise; explicit systems own state, policy, and replay.
- **Evidence before claims.** Benchmarks, artifacts, and failure cases matter more than impressive-sounding numbers.
- **Documentation as part of the system.** Architecture, operational boundaries, and validation paths evolve with the code.
